Our Vision

Elevating Giridih’s Urban Horizon

Ninety Nine Builders & Developers (99Realty) was founded with a definitive mission: to bring institutional-grade architectural planning, modern lifestyle amenities, and rigorous legal transparency to tier-2 cities like Giridih.

For decades, real estate across Giridih suffered from fragmented development, unmonitored timelines, and lack of lifestyle amenities. Through landmark projects like 99Exotica in Babhantoli, we introduced Giridih’s first residential community featuring a rooftop infinity pool, a dedicated clubhouse, landscaped terrace gardens, and an authentic community temple.

Simultaneously, with 99 Tower at Bada Chowk, we are constructing state-of-the-art commercial and retail infrastructure that provides national brands, financial institutions, and medical professionals with Grade-A corporate environments.

“We don’t just build structures of concrete and steel; we deliver legally bulletproof assets backed by RERA escrow protection and lifelong trust.”
