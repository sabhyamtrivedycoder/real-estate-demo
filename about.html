Why Vikash Kumar Sinha Started Giridih Real Estate

In small towns like Giridih, buying a home or a plot was historically filled with confusion: middlemen charging hefty commissions, unverified ancestral paperwork leading to courtroom disputes, and zero transparency.

Vikash Kumar Sinha pioneered a direct developer-to-buyer model. By working directly with top local builders and verified freehold land owners, he completely eliminated brokerage fees for buyers, saving families anywhere from ₹1,00,000 to ₹3,00,000 on their life savings.
