Our Protocol

The 4-Stage Legal Verification Framework

How we protect every rupee you invest in Giridih real estate.

01

30-Year Deed Audit

We examine registered title deeds, parent deeds, and link documents through the Giridih Sub-Registrar to ensure unbroken chain of ownership.

02

Revenue & CNT Compliance

We cross-verify Khatiyan classifications at the Anchal Adhikari (Circle Office) level to guarantee the land is not CNT restricted or tribal tenancy land.

03

Boundary & Road Survey

We physically inspect the physical boundaries, road access width, electricity poles, and drainage channels with certified local Amins.

04

Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij)

Our office tracks your mutation file until the official online Lagan receipt is generated on the Jharbhoomi portal in the buyer’s name.
