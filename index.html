The Four Pillars of Real Estate Diligence

Every property advised through our desk adheres to strict legal and revenue protocols in Jharkhand.

01

100% Registry Verification

We personally inspect certified deed copies, Khatian lineage, and encumbrance records at the Giridih sub-registrar office before listing.

02

Direct Landowner Meetings

We do not operate through unverified secondary brokers. Buyers negotiate directly with original title holders for complete price transparency.

03

Complimentary Site Vehicle

Our dedicated office vehicle provides door-to-site pickup from Giridih Railway Station or your residence for thorough neighborhood inspections.

04

Post-Purchase Revenue Support

We assist our clients with Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation) formalities, electricity connection transfer, and municipal tax registrations.
