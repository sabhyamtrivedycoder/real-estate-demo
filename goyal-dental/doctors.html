Patient Safety First

Our 6-Stage Sterilization Protocol

We follow strict international infection-control protocols to ensure 100% sterile cross-contamination prevention.

01

Enzymatic Decontamination

Used surgical and diagnostic instruments are immediately soaked in enzymatic disinfectant solutions to break down organic bio-burden.

02

Ultrasonic Cavitation Scrub

Instruments undergo automated high-frequency ultrasonic scrub cycles that remove micro-debris from intricate hinge joints and serrations.

03

Hermetic Pouch Packaging

Cleaned instruments are individually wrapped and heat-sealed in medical-grade sterilization pouches with chemical color-change indicator strips.

04

Class-B Vacuum Autoclave

Pouches are sterilized under high pressure saturated steam at 134°C in a fractional pre-vacuum Class-B autoclave, killing 100% of resistant spores.

05

Chairside Unsealing

Your individual sterile instrument pouch is cut open directly in your presence before treatment commences, ensuring complete peace of mind.

06

Surface Barrier Films

Dental chair switches, light handles, and suction units are wrapped in single-use protective barriers and wiped with hospital-grade disinfectant after every patient.
