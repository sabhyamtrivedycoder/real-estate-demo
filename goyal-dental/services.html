Implantology Permanent Solution

Titanium Dental Implants

The international gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A surgical-grade pure titanium post is placed into the jawbone to replicate the natural tooth root, upon which an abutment and custom CAD/CAM zirconia crown is fitted.

  • Preserves jawbone density and facial structure
  • No grinding or damage to neighboring healthy teeth
  • Lifetime clinical longevity with proper oral maintenance

Painless Root Canal Therapy (RCT)

Severely decayed or infected teeth are rescued through micro-endodontic rotary cleaning. Infected pulp tissue is gently removed, canals are sanitized with ultrasonic irrigation, and sealed with bio-ceramic obturation.

  • 100% painless local anesthesia delivery
  • Apex locator precision for complete root disinfection
  • Topped with precision-milled Zirconia or Ceramic crowns
