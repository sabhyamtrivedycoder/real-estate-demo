Clinical Spectrum

Conditions Successfully Corrected

Over 20 years of clinical cases in Gurugram successfully diagnosed and corrected.

Dental Crowding

When there is insufficient room in the jawbone for teeth to erupt normally, leading to overlapping, twisted, or displaced teeth that trap food and promote decay.

Solution: Clear Aligners / Arch Expansion

Spaced Teeth (Diastema)

Noticeable gaps between front or side teeth caused by missing teeth, undersized teeth, abnormal frenum attachments, or tongue-thrusting habits.

Solution: Rapid Gap Closure via Aligners

Deep Overbite

Upper front teeth overlap the lower front teeth excessively, often causing lower teeth to bite into the upper palate and accelerating enamel attrition.

Solution: Intrusion & Bite Ramp Alignment

Underbite & Reverse Bite

Lower jaw protrudes forward, causing the lower front teeth to rest outside the upper front teeth, impacting chewing efficiency and speech.

Solution: Intermaxillary Biomechanics

Crossbite

One or more upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth rather than outside, causing asymmetrical jaw growth, facial imbalance, and TMJ joint pain.

Solution: Transverse Dental Correction

Open Bite

Front upper and lower teeth do not touch when back teeth are closed together, leaving an opening that impairs speech clarity and biting food.

Solution: Molar Intrusion & Anterior Closure
Clinical Advisory

Why In-Person MDS Orthodontic Supervision is Vital

Recent mail-order direct-to-consumer clear aligner advertisements promise "teeth straightening from home" without in-person doctor visits. The Indian Orthodontic Society (IOS) and dental regulatory bodies strongly caution against unmonitored aligners.

Moving teeth involves dynamic biological bone remodeling. Without direct periodontal evaluation, roots can suffer irreversible resorption, gum recession, and even loose teeth. At Goyal Dental, Dr. Vinod Goyal (BDS, MDS) personally conducts routine monthly clinical evaluations to ensure your roots and bone remain 100% healthy throughout alignment.
