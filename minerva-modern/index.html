Director's Manifesto

"No student's academic future should be derailed by one bad exam season."

When I established Minerva Academy in Gurugram in 1982 following my studies at Osmania University, Hyderabad, private open schooling was virtually unknown in Haryana. Students who failed 10th or 12th were treated as failures for life.

Over four decades, our single mission has been to provide genuine, recognized alternative educational channels through the National Institute of Open Schooling and premier distance universities. We operate with strict adherence to law, ethical counsel, and zero fake boards.

Institutional Integrity

Every student is registered exclusively on government-notified national boards and recognized universities.

Individual Mentorship
