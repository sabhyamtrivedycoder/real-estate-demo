Admissions Guide & Required Documents

A clear, step-by-step documentation manual to ensure your admission to NIOS, UGC distance degree programs, or law courses is processed accurately without procedural rejections.

Documentation Manual

Required Documents by Program

Please ensure all original documents along with two sets of self-attested photocopies are kept ready.

NIOS Secondary (10th)

10th Standard Admission

  • Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation OR
  • School Leaving Certificate (TC) showing 8th/9th standard
  • Original Aadhaar Card (with correct date of birth)
  • 4 Passport size photographs with white background
NIOS Sr. Secondary (12th)

12th Standard Admission

  • Original 10th Standard Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Minimum 1-year academic gap after passing 10th
  • Original Aadhaar Card
  • 4 Passport size photographs with white background
Failed Candidates

Stream 2 / ODE with TOC

  • Original Failed / Compartment Marksheet (CBSE/ICSE/HBSE)
  • Board Admit Card / Hall Ticket
  • Original Aadhaar Card
  • 4 Passport size photographs
Distance Degree

BA / B.Com / BCA

  • 10th and 12th Standard Marksheets & Certificates
  • Migration Certificate from previous board (original)
  • Aadhaar Card and Caste Certificate (if seeking quota)
  • 6 Passport size photographs
Law Admission

3-Year Professional LLB

  • All Semester/Year Marksheets of Bachelor Degree (min 45%)
  • Provisional / Degree Certificate & University Migration
  • 10th & 12th passing certificates (for age verification)
  • Aadhaar Card and 6 Passport photographs
Postgraduate

MA / MBA / M.Com

  • Bachelor Degree Marksheets and Degree Certificate
  • University Migration Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card and address proof
  • 4 Passport size photographs

Public Advisory on Unrecognized Private Boards

In recent years, numerous unverified commercial shops have surfaced advertising "10th/12th in 30 days" or "One sitting degree" through fake autonomous boards or derecognized private bodies. Certificates from such institutions are invalid for government employment, passport verification, police verification, or higher education admissions.

Minerva Academy strictly enrolls candidates into the National Institute of Open Schooling (MHRD Govt of India) and UGC-DEB approved state/central universities. We do not deal in any unaccredited or non-notified certifications.
