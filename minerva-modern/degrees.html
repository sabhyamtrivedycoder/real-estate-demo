Full Parity with Conventional Degrees

Pursuant to the University Grants Commission (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations and subsequent official Gazette Notifications, degrees acquired through open and distance learning mode from UGC-DEB recognized higher educational institutions are treated on an equal footing with corresponding degrees earned through traditional classroom mode for all government services, higher education admissions, and private employment.
