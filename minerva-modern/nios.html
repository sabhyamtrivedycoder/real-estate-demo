Academic Relief

Understanding Transfer of Credit (TOC)

If you have appeared for 10th or 12th examinations from CBSE, ICSE, Haryana Board (BSEH), UP Board, or any recognized state board and passed in at least 1 or 2 subjects but could not pass the overall exam, you do not need to restart from scratch.

TOC Qualification Criteria:

  • Maximum of 2 passed subjects can be transferred to NIOS.
  • Original failed marksheet must be submitted to NIOS regional authority.
  • Marks are directly reflected on your consolidated NIOS final marksheet.

Validity Disclosure

Legal Standing & Equivalence of NIOS

The National Institute of Open Schooling is an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Education (MHRD), Government of India. As per Association of Indian Universities (AIU) notification and Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) rules, NIOS certificates are 100% equivalent to CBSE, ICSE, and state boards for all university admissions, competitive examinations (NEET, JEE, NDA, CLAT), and central/state government employment.
