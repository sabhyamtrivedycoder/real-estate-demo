The Founder's Journey

Bhoop Singh (B. S. Chaudhary)

Founder & Managing Director • Alumnus of Osmania University, Hyderabad

In 1982, when education in Gurgaon was largely restricted to conventional, rigid schooling with little support for struggling or working students, Bhoop Singh Chaudhary established Minerva Academy in New Colony.

His founding principle was straightforward yet radical: failure in an examination should never mark the end of an individual’s educational or professional future. Over the past 42 years, B. S. Chaudhary has personally counseled and mentored more than 15,000 students through open schooling (NIOS), helping young individuals overcome board exam failures, enter college, and establish successful careers in civil services, armed forces, law, and corporate management.

As Gurugram evolved from a rural agricultural township into an international IT and financial hub, Minerva Academy expanded its guidance into recognized distance bachelor’s degrees (BA, B.Com, BBA), Bar Council of India approved LLB programs, and executive master’s degrees to support the booming workforce of Delhi NCR.

“Our duty is not merely to get a student enrolled, but to walk with them through every assignment, every syllabus doubt, and every practical until the day their degree is in their hands.”
— Bhoop Singh Chaudhary

Academic Ethics

The Minerva Ethical Code

In an education consulting sector frequently marred by bogus boards, we maintain uncompromising transparency.

Zero Unrecognized Boards

We categorically refuse to associate with fake private boards or unverified distance centers. Every marksheet guided through our desk comes from official government councils.

Honest Eligibility Advice

If a student is not eligible for a specific program due to age or document gaps, we state it honestly rather than taking fees and making false guarantees.

Continuous Mentorship

Our support does not terminate upon admission fee payment. We maintain direct communication with students until examinations, practicals, and marksheets are concluded.
