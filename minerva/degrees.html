Professional Legal Education

LLB Admission Counseling & Guidance

Minerva Academy provides premier admission assistance for Law aspirants in Bar Council of India (BCI) recognized universities. Whether you are a fresh 12th pass student or an experienced corporate professional seeking a legal degree, we guide you to the right institution.

3-Year LLB (Bachelor of Legislative Law)
For candidates who have completed graduation in any discipline (BA, B.Com, B.Sc, BBA, B.Tech) with minimum 45% marks. Fully eligible for Bar Council enrollment and court practice.
5-Year Integrated Law (BA LLB / BBA LLB)
Direct dual-degree program after 12th standard. Covers foundational humanities or management alongside procedural and constitutional law.
Consult With Director for LLB Admission

Career Paths for Law Graduates

  • Court Practice & Advocacy: Enrollment with Bar Council of Delhi/Punjab & Haryana.
  • Judicial Services: Eligibility for Civil Judge (Junior Division) PCS-J examinations.
  • Corporate Legal Counsel: Compliance manager and in-house legal advisor in Gurugram MNCs.
  • Public Prosecutor & Legal Officer: Banking (IBPS SO Law) and PSU legal advisor roles.
