Preparation Checklist

Required Documents for Property Loan in Jharkhand

Keep these documents ready to ensure quick legal appraisal and sanction by Giridih lenders.

1. Legal Property Papers

  • • Registered Title Deed (Sale Deed copy)
  • • Link deeds going back 30 years
  • • Latest Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation) receipt
  • • Non-Encumbrance Certificate (NEC)
  • • Approved Building Map (for flats)

2. Salaried Applicants

  • • Last 3 months payslips
  • • Form 16 / ITR for past 2 assessment years
  • • 6 months salary bank account statement
  • • Pan Card & Aadhaar Card
  • • Employee ID card & address proof

3. Self-Employed / Business

  • • 3 years ITR with computation of income
  • • CA-certified Balance Sheet & P&L
  • • 12 months current bank account statement
  • • GST registration certificate
  • • Business establishment license
